# Lowkey 0.15.0

## Changed

- More words suggest an emoji. In English, a word from an emoji's Unicode name now offers that emoji, so **thumb** suggests 👍 and **pancakes** 🥞. Plurals work too, also for the short keyword list (**cars** suggests 🚗). A word only counts where it names the picture, so **medical** does not suggest a face with a medical mask. Symbols, flags, very common words and words shorter than four letters are left out, so word suggestions stay in place while you type.
- More keywords, such as **water**, **ball**, **money**, **beach**, **cheese**, **wine** and **map** in English, and **duim** and **auto** in Dutch.

This update preserves your settings, saved words and downloaded models.
