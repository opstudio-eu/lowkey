# Lowkey 0.14.0

## Changed

- More words suggest an emoji. In English, a word from an emoji's Unicode name now offers that emoji, so **thumb** suggests 👍 and **pancakes** 🥞. Plurals work too. Symbols, flags, very common words and words shorter than four letters are left out, so word suggestions stay in place while you type.
- A few more keywords: **water**, **ball**, **snow**, **running**, **food**, **computer**, **flower** and **moon** in English, and **duim** and **auto** in Dutch.

This update preserves your settings, saved words and downloaded models.
