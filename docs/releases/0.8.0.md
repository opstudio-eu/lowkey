# Lowkey 0.8.0

## Added

- Toggle the existing number-row setting directly from the keyboard's quick menu. The menu shows On/Off captions and checkmarks for active modes and settings.
- Get word suggestions from up to three enabled, installed languages without changing the physical keyboard layout. Words recognized in another participating language are protected from cross-language autocorrection; ambiguous corrections preserve what you typed.
- See offline emoji suggestions for supported English and Dutch words, such as a waving hand for "hi" or "hoi". Tap to insert the emoji; Space keeps its usual word behavior.

## Changed

- Resize is now called Size & position. Dictionary management remains available in the companion app; its quick-menu tile is replaced by Number row.
- Hide the small number hints on letter keys while the dedicated number row is enabled, including on AZERTY. Long-press number shortcuts still work.
- Choose accented characters from a compact grid of at most five columns. Slide to select an alternative or move away to cancel.

## Updating

Install the signed `lowkey.apk` over the existing Lowkey app, or use **Settings → Updates → Check now**. Settings, downloaded models and the personal dictionary remain in place.
