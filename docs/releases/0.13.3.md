# Lowkey 0.13.3

## Improved

- **Speech models** now have their own page under **Voice**. Each model shows whether it is active, on your phone or not downloaded. From there you can switch with **Use**, download another model, cancel a download, or delete a model you no longer need. The page also shows how much space models use.
- During voice setup, **Choose another model** opens the same page, next to the one-tap download of the recommended model.
- The keyboard's **Manage models** button, the Storage page and Settings search now open the Speech models page.

This update preserves your settings, saved words and downloaded models.
