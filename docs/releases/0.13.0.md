# Lowkey 0.13.0

## New

- **Search settings** at the top of the Settings tab finds any setting by its name or an everyday word, such as *dark* for Theme or *haptic* for key vibration. It covers keyboard, voice, dictionary, theme and update settings.
- Tapping a result opens the setting's page, scrolls to the setting and briefly highlights it. **Back** returns to your results.

This update preserves your settings, saved words and downloaded models.
