# Lowkey 0.13.1

## Changed

- Getting back from emoji is easier. The emoji panel now sits over the keys like a sheet, with the top of the bottom row peeking out below it. The key you opened emoji with shows there as a lit keyboard tab: tap it, or anywhere on that strip, to return to letters. Swiping down on the category bar or the strip works too. The small **ABC** label at the top left is gone, leaving more room for categories.
- Emoji are sized so whole rows fill the panel when a category opens.

This update preserves your settings, saved words and downloaded models.
