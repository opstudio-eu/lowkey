# Lowkey 0.13.1

## Changed

- Getting back from emoji is easier. A keyboard key now sits exactly where the key you opened emoji with was, so the same spot takes you back to letters. Swiping down on the category bar works too. The small **ABC** label at the top left is gone, leaving more room for categories.
- All emoji scroll in one list, starting with your recent ones. Tap a category icon to jump to it; the icon of the section you are in stays highlighted.

This update preserves your settings, saved words and downloaded models.
