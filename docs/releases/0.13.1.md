# Lowkey 0.13.1

## Changed

- Getting back from emoji is easier. An **ABC** tab with a keyboard icon now sits in the bottom-left corner of the emoji panel, joined to the keyboard's edge, so it is easy to find wherever the keyboard is placed. It replaces the small **ABC** label at the top left, which leaves more room for categories. Swiping down on the category bar works too.
- All emoji scroll in one list, starting with your recent ones. Tap a category icon to jump to it; the icon of the section you are in stays highlighted.

This update preserves your settings, saved words and downloaded models.
