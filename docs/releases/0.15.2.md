# Lowkey 0.15.2

## New

- **Emoji search.** Tap the magnifier at the start of the emoji panel's category bar. The letter keys then type into a search box instead of your text, and the row above them shows matching emoji, best first. Search knows emoji names and keywords in English and, when Dutch is one of your suggestion languages, Dutch: **heart**, **lol**, **red hea**, **cars**, **fiets**, **duim**. It also takes an emoticon such as **;)**. Before you type, the row shows your recent emoji. **‹**, Done, the smiley key or Back returns to all emoji.
- **Emoticons suggest their emoji.** Right after **;)**, **:)**, **:D**, **xD**, **:(**, **:P**, **<3** and others, the right-hand suggestion offers the matching emoji; tapping it replaces the emoticon. This also works when the keyboard has just moved a space behind the punctuation after a tapped suggestion.

## Improved

- **More emoji suggestions while typing.** **wink** (or **knipoog**) offers 😉. In Dutch, a word that is an emoji's whole Dutch name now offers it, such as **fiets** 🚲 or **olifant** 🐘; everyday words such as **elf**, **man** or **beer** stay plain words.

This update preserves your settings, saved words and downloaded models.
