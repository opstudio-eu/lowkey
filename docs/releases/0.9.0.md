# Lowkey 0.9.0

## Added

- Give the keyboard its own colours in **Settings → Appearance → Keyboard theme**. Choose five main colours with sliders or hex codes, override any derived colour under **Advanced colors**, and add a dimmed background image from the photo picker. A preview shows the draft, and contrast warnings point out hard-to-read text with a suggested fix.
- The custom colours apply to every part of the keyboard: keys, suggestions, the emoji panel, the quick menu, the voice row, and the docked, one-handed, split and floating layouts.

## Changed

- The navigation bar under the keyboard picks light or dark icons from the keyboard's actual background, including a background image. While the keyboard floats, the bar stays transparent.

## Updating

Install the signed `lowkey.apk` over the existing Lowkey app, or use **Settings → Updates → Check now**. Settings, downloaded models and the personal dictionary remain in place. The keyboard keeps its standard colours until you choose **Custom**.
