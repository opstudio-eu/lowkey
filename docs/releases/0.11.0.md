# Lowkey 0.11.0

## Typing improvements

- The comma now sits to the left of the emoji or language key, with that key
  directly beside Space.
- Letters with accented alternatives have a small corner mark, including when
  the dedicated number row is visible.
- Press Space twice quickly after a word to insert a period and a space.
  Immediate Backspace restores the space. The shortcut stays off in password,
  address, numeric and private fields.
- Safe contraction fixes and spelling corrections remain available alongside
  word completions instead of being crowded out.
- After a space, suggestions can reuse wording from the current field or saved
  multiword dictionary terms. This looks at up to 2,048 preceding characters
  without storing them. It is limited phrase matching, not a general language
  model, and stays off in private and address fields.

## Updating

Install the signed `lowkey.apk` over the existing Lowkey app, or use
**Settings → Updates → Check now**. Settings, languages, downloaded models and
the personal dictionary remain in place.
