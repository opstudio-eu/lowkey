## The app

Lowkey is an Android keyboard with on-device suggestions, autocorrect and voice typing, and an optional experimental feature that can send a prompt to an AI provider you choose.

The app is provided so you can type. It can be wrong. A suggestion, a correction, a transcription or an image from the experimental feature may not be what you wanted. Read text before you send it, and treat an AI image as untrusted output.

The published package Op Studio intends to ship is `com.opstudio.lowkey`. It replaces the retired package `com.rheerkens.androidkeyboard`. Android treats that as a different app.

## Experimental AI

The feature is off until you turn it on. You choose OpenAI or an OpenAI-compatible server, and you supply the key or token. The key stays on your phone. The prompt and any pictures you submit go from your phone to that provider. Op Studio is not a party to the provider's terms and does not see the request.

You are responsible for the key, for keeping it on a phone you trust, and for the charges the provider makes when the app uses it. Someone who can use your unlocked phone can start a request that spends that account. You can delete the key in Settings → Experimental.

The provider may refuse a request under its own rules. Lowkey does not add a separate moderation call. Output can be wrong, incomplete or offensive. You decide whether to use it.

Your use of the provider has to follow that provider's terms. Do not send a prompt or a picture you have no right to send.
