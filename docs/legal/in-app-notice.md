## English

Lowkey is published by Op Studio.

Typing, suggestions, autocorrect and voice transcription stay on this phone. Lowkey does connect to the internet in these cases: to download a speech model or a keyboard language you choose; to check for an update, and to download an update you ask for; and, if you turn on experimental AI, to send the prompt and any pictures you submit to the AI provider you choose, with your own key. If that provider returns a link to the finished image, the phone downloads that link.

The hosts that deliver a download, and an AI provider you choose, can see the connection from this phone. Op Studio does not receive what you type, say or store in the app. There is no account, no advertising and no analytics.
